# Welcome to SELAH 🌿

**Pause. Reflect. Grow.**

SELAH is a Bible app for reading Scripture, writing reflections, and keeping a
private prayer journal. No account is required.

This walkthrough covers version **0.2.0**.

## 1. Install or open SELAH

- **Android:** [Download SELAH v0.2.0](https://github.com/jhombalz/selah/releases/download/v0.2.0/selah-v0.2.0-android.apk).
  If Android asks, allow installation from the browser you used. Open the
  downloaded APK and follow the installation instructions.
- **Website:** [Open SELAH](https://jhombalz.github.io/selah/).

On Android, the included Cebuano Bible is available offline immediately after
installation. On the website, first open the app with internet and allow its
offline preparation to finish before relying on offline access.

## 2. Start reading

Tap **Bible** in the navigation menu.

- Tap the **book-and-chapter button** to choose a book and chapter.
- Books are grouped into **Old Testament** and **New Testament**, with categories
  such as Law, History, Poetry & Wisdom, and Gospels.
- Use the **previous and next arrows** to move between chapters.
- Tap **AA** to adjust text size.
- Scrolling down hides the reading controls to give Scripture more space.
  Scroll up to show them again.

The Cebuano Bible is included. Open **Settings → Bible translations** to find
other available translations. Once downloaded, they can be read offline.

Home has a **Continue Reading** button to return to where you paused. Mark a
chapter as read when you choose; simply opening it does not mark it complete.

## 3. Save something meaningful

Long-press a verse and drag to select a verse range. You can:

- Bookmark the passage.
- Highlight it with a chosen color.
- Write a personal note.

A **small note icon** identifies verses with a reflection. Tap it to open the
note. Find saved passages under **Saved**.

Use **Search** to look for words, phrases, or a reference such as `John 3:16`.
Search works with the Bible translation stored on your device.

## 4. Try “My time with God”

From Home, open **My time with God**. You can also reach it through **Saved →
My time with God & prayers**.

Begin with your current chapter, or choose an optional theme:

- Anxiety
- Stress
- Grief
- Loneliness
- Joy

Follow the guided moment at your own pace:

1. **Pause:** Take a breath and prepare to listen.
2. **Read:** Open the passage in the normal Bible reader, with the full chapter
   available. Tap **Continue reflection** to return to your guided session.
3. **Reflect:** Write an observation, question, or honest response.
4. **Pray:** Use the suggested starting point or write in your own words.
5. **Live:** Choose one small response to carry into your day, if you wish.

Every step is optional. Drafts save automatically, so you can return later.
Completing a guided moment does not automatically mark the Bible chapter read.

The included theme guides are original English pilot content. They are
reflection guidance, not additional Scripture, and have not had independent
pastoral review. The Bible text remains in your selected translation.

## 5. Keep a prayer journal

Inside **My time with God**, tap **+** to write a prayer.

- Give it a title and choose a category: Request, Gratitude, Lament, or Waiting.
- Write your prayer. Your draft saves locally.
- Add dated updates later as you return to it.
- Choose your own status: **Still praying**, **Answered**, or **Archived**.

Only you choose a prayer’s status. SELAH does not decide whether or how a
prayer was answered.

## 6. Explore optional features

### Verse of the Day

Home offers a daily verse in your selected translation. When available, an
online service supplies a random reference. Without internet, SELAH can choose
from the Bible stored locally. The day’s choice is saved for reopening.
Tap **Read in context** to open its passage.

### Audio

Select **WEB** or **BSB**, then use the **Play** button beside the chapter bar.
Recorded narration requires internet. Audio is not configured for every
translation; SELAH does not substitute another edition’s recording.

### Study notes

While reading the Bible, tap a verse and choose **Study notes**. You can also
select a verse range first. The notes are **Tyndale Open Study Notes in English**,
even if your Bible text is Cebuano or Tagalog. They are commentary, separate from
Scripture and your personal reflections.

Choose **Selected passage** for matching notes or **Whole chapter** for nearby
context. Some passages have no notes. The first opening of a chapter needs
internet; opened chapters are saved for offline use, up to 100 recent chapters.
Use **Refresh** to check for updates, or **Remove offline copy** to remove that
chapter's cached commentary. Personal writing is not removed. **Source & license**
shows attribution and opens the publisher and CC BY-SA license links.

### Bible assessments

On Home, open **Bible assessments**. Level 1 is available first. Each level has
20 English multiple-choice questions; score **80% (16/20)** to pass and unlock
the next level. The time limits are 3 hours, 2½ hours, 2 hours, 1½ hours and 1 hour.

Read the warning before starting: **the timer cannot be paused**, even when you
leave or close SELAH. Answers save as you choose them. You can revisit questions
and flag them for review. Submit when ready, or let the deadline automatically
submit saved answers. Unanswered questions count as incorrect.

Results show your grade and topic scores without showing correct choices. Retake
the same level **24 hours after submission**. A newly unlocked level can be
started immediately. Only one attempt can be active. Progress is device-local
and is not part of Bible backups. This score does not measure your faith.

### Speak your reflection — Android

In **My time with God**, open a guided session and go to **Reflect**. Tap
**Speak my reflection**, select English, Filipino/Tagalog, or Cebuano if your
phone supports it, then speak using Android's speech dialog. Review and correct
the text, then tap **Add to reflection**. It appends to your existing writing
and autosaves. Discarding or cancelling leaves your reflection unchanged.

The phone's speech service may need internet and may send audio to its provider.
SELAH does not save recordings. If voice input is unavailable, use keyboard
dictation or type as usual. Supported languages depend on your device.

### Daily reminders — Android

Inside **My time with God**, find **Morning verse reminder** and **Before sleep**.
Enable either or both, choose your times, and allow notifications if Android asks.
The initial times are 8:00 AM and 9:00 PM. Tap an enabled reminder's time to
change it. Tap a notification to open the Bible. These reminders work offline.

The reminder contains no private prayer or journal text. Android may delay
delivery to save battery. Reminders are not available on the website.

### Focus Mode — Android

Open **Settings → Focus Mode**:

1. Choose your daily Bible-reading time.
2. Select the apps you want to pause.
3. Enable Focus Mode, review its explanation, and enable **SELAH Focus Mode**
   in Android Accessibility settings.

Opening a selected app before completing your goal shows a reading gate.
Tap **Read in SELAH** to open the Bible reader and continue your reading time.

Time counts only while the eligible Bible reader is visible and the screen is
unlocked. Leaving the reader, opening a dialog, or writing in guided reflection
and prayer pages pauses the timer. Completing the goal unlocks the selected
apps for the rest of the day; the goal resets at local midnight.

Focus Mode is optional and can be disabled. Reading minutes are not a measure
of faith or closeness to God.

### Sharing

Use the share icon in a guided session or prayer, or choose **Share passage**
from a verse’s menu.

1. Check the content you want to include.
2. Read the preview.
3. Tap **Share** and choose an app and recipient, or use **Copy**.

Private reflection, prayer, and response fields start unchecked. Nothing is
sent automatically.

## 7. Protect your writing

Open **Settings → Backup & restore → Export backup** regularly.

Backups include notes, bookmarks, highlights, reading history, preferences,
guided sessions, and prayers. Bible texts and downloaded translations are not
included.

Your writing stays on that device or browser. **Android and the website do not
automatically sync.** To transfer your writing, export a backup from one and
restore it on the other. Download any additional Bible translations separately.

- **Merge** keeps existing local conflicts and combines supported records.
- **Replace** removes local records absent from the selected backup. Review the
  preview and confirmation carefully, especially when using an older backup
  that contains no journals.

Backup files contain private writing and are **unencrypted**. Keep them
somewhere safe. Clearing app data or browser site data can remove local records.

## A simple first visit

Read one chapter, write one honest reflection, and bring one concern to God in
prayer. There is no streak you need to maintain.

**SELAH — Pause. Reflect. Grow.**
